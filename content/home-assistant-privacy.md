This policy covers one thing: the Google OAuth app called **Home Assistant**, which connects
Google Calendar to my own [Home Assistant](https://www.home-assistant.io) server at home. It is not
a public service. I am its only user, and it signs in to my own Google accounts.

## What it accesses

Read-only access to my Google calendars (`calendar.readonly`): calendar names, and event titles,
times, descriptions and locations. It cannot create, change or delete anything.

## Where the data goes

From Google to my Home Assistant server, which keeps a local copy to show my upcoming events at
home. It is not sent anywhere else, shared with or sold to anyone, used for advertising, or used to
train any model.

## How long it is kept

Only while the connection exists. Removing the integration from Home Assistant deletes the local
copy, and the access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's
[third-party connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
